    Our story

    From the mills of Biella to the streets of everywhere.

    FILA began in 1911 in Biella, at the foot of the Italian Alps, weaving textiles for the people of the valley. Decades later, that obsession with craft found its way onto tennis courts, ski slopes and basketball floors, dressing the champions of each era.

    Then the street took over. The Disruptor turned a running sole into a statement, and a generation wore it louder than anyone expected. Today we still design with the same idea: heritage you can feel, proportions you can't ignore.
